What we study

Research

Fundamental advances in trustworthy machine learning, connected to health, accessibility, and society.

Large Language Model Safety and SecurityLarge Language Model UnlearningLarge Vision-Language Model for Grounded Spatial ReasoningVision–Language Models for Medical Image SegmentationOn-device machine learningSmart and connected healthFairness-aware decision support systemAutomatic medical imaging interpretationExplainable recommender systemSpatial-temporal modeling of 4D CNN for fMRI analysisContext-aware conversational agent

01 / Research direction

Large Language Model Safety and Security

We study attacks on large language models and develop defenses that improve safety, alignment, and robustness.

02 / Research direction

Large Language Model Unlearning

We develop methods that help language models forget unwanted or sensitive information while preserving useful knowledge.

03 / Research direction

Large Vision-Language Model for Grounded Spatial Reasoning

We combine vision, language, and segmentation to support spatial reasoning and accessible pedestrian navigation.

04 / Research direction

Vision–Language Models for Medical Image Segmentation

We connect medical images with clinical language to improve segmentation and cross-modal reasoning.

05 / Research direction

On-device machine learning

We build efficient, privacy-aware AI for language and medical imaging on mobile and resource-limited devices.

06 / Research direction

Smart and connected health

We use spatial and temporal IoT data for prediction, representation learning, and anomaly detection in connected health.

07 / Research direction

Fairness-aware decision support system

We design decision-support methods that address sampling, social, and algorithmic biases.

08 / Research direction

Automatic medical imaging interpretation

We combine visual and language features to help AI interpret medical images.

09 / Research direction

Explainable recommender system

We use domain knowledge to make personalized recommendations easier to explain.

10 / Research direction

Spatial-temporal modeling of 4D CNN for fMRI analysis

We develop 4D convolutional models to learn developmental patterns from fMRI data.

11 / Research direction

Context-aware conversational agent

We develop conversational agents that use context and location to generate relevant responses.
